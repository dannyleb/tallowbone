# The Canal District, as ASCII. tools/build_level.py turns this into
# scenes/levels/canal_district.tscn (tile layers + placed props and enemies).
# Once you edit that scene in Godot, the scene is the source of truth;
# re-running the builder overwrites it.
#
# Legend:
#   #  stone (solid)            =  walkway (one-way: jump up through it,
#   V  veil (solid until burnt      down + jump to drop through)
#      by the Lantern form)     ~  canal water (hurts, sends you back)
#   P  player start             L  street lamp (checkpoint, levelling)
#   c  Communicant              h  Hound
#   v  vial holder              b  bullet pouch
#   1-9  signs (text in [signs])

[name]
The Canal District

[signs]
1 = {move} to walk.  {jump} to jump. Hold it to jump higher.
2 = {attack} to strike, again to keep the combo going.  {transform} turns the Pole into its Lantern form. Mid-combo, the turn is a trick attack.
3 = {dash} to quickstep. Nothing can touch you mid-step, and it carries you over gaps (once per jump).
4 = Hurt? Part of your health lingers, pale. Strike back fast and you win it back: Rally.  {heal} drinks a vial.
5 = {shoot} fires the pistol. Shoot an enemy the instant it glints and it staggers, then strike to finish it.  Out of bullets? Hold {shoot} to spend blood on more.
6 = A pale veil seals the meeting-hall door. Light burns it: strike it in Lantern form ({transform}).
7 = The church doors are barred from inside. The Long Night goes on... (end of the slice)
