# re-running the builder overwrites it.

# Legend:
#   #  stone (solid)            =  walkway (one-way: jump up through it,
#   V  veil (solid until burnt      down + jump to drop through)
#      by the Lantern form)     ~  canal water (hurts, sends you back)
#   P  player start             L  street lamp (checkpoint, levelling)
#   c  Communicant              h  Hound
#   v  vial holder              b  bullet pouch
#   d  Watchman's Boots (quickstep)    g  Watch Pistol
#   o  Lamp Oil (the Lantern form)     i  Climbing Irons (wall jump)
#   ]  barred gate, opened from its right (east) side; [ from its left.
#      Mark the bottom cell; it stands three tiles tall.
#   1-9  signs (text in [signs])

[name]
The Canal District

[signs]
1 = {move} to walk.  {jump} to jump. Hold it to jump higher.
2 = {attack} to strike. Strike again to keep the combo going.
3 = The canal is cold and deep. Take the mooring posts: {jump} from the edge.
4 = Hurt? Part of your health lingers, pale. Strike back fast to win it back: Rally.  {heal} drinks a vial.
5 = Communicants glint just before they strike. Shoot one at that instant ({shoot}) and it staggers. Then strike to finish it.
6 = A pale veil seals the meeting-hall door. Light burns it: hold {attack} to turn the Pole into its Lantern, then strike.
7 = The church doors are barred from inside. The Long Night goes on... (end of the slice)
8 = A chimney climbs into the dark. Too sheer to climb barehanded.
